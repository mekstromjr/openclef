# Sharing

Your library is private until you share it. Sharing works like a photo app's partner sharing: you choose people, and they see all of your music.

## Sharing your library

1. Open **Settings** from your account menu.
2. Under **Sharing**, choose a person in **I share my library with**, then **Share**.

They can now find, play and edit everything you own, and put it on their setlists. They can't delete your music or pass it on to anyone else.

Sharing is one way. Sharing with someone doesn't let you see their music; they have to share with you too. And it doesn't chain: if you share with Sam and Sam shares with Alex, Alex sees Sam's music, not yours.

To stop, choose **Stop sharing** next to their name. Your pieces leave their library and stop opening from their setlists.

## Music shared with you

Pieces someone else shares with you appear in your library with a small people icon. Hover over it, or open the piece, to see who shared it.

Under **Shared with me** in Settings:

- **Show in my library** decides whether that person's music appears in your main library. Turning it off doesn't remove anything: it stays on their page and in your setlists.
- The person's name opens their page: everything they share with you, with the same search and filters as your library.

## Only my music

**Only my music** at the top of the library hides everything shared with you, so you see just the pieces you added.

## Deleting a shared piece

Only the person who owns a piece, or an admin, can delete it, and not while it is on a setlist. If someone you share with put it on one of their setlists, the piece's page says how many setlists you can't see are holding it. Ask them to remove it first.
