# Imagery

## System icons

- **Family:** Lucide (primary). Phosphor "regular" weight as secondary fallback.
- **Stroke:** 1.75 px at 24 px size; scales linearly.
- **Color:** Inherits `--color-clef-stone-300` by default; switches to
  `--color-clef-claret` on active/selected state.
- Avoid mixing icon families inside a single view.

## Photography

- **Subject:** Real instruments, real printed scores, real practice rooms.
  People are welcome but never the focus — the focus is the page on the stand.
- **Light:** Warm, natural, side-lit. Soft shadows. Window light is the ideal.
- **Color:** Slightly desaturated, with paper shifted toward Ivory. Avoid
  blue-cool color grading.
- **Avoid:** Stock-music tropes (fluttering sheet music, hands holding glowing
  tablets, generic concert-hall stage lights), AI-rendered instruments with
  extra strings, anything that screams "tech startup."

## Illustration

When iconography isn't enough, lean on **annotated score fragments** — a
snippet of staff with a Claret marking-pencil note — over freeform
illustration. The score itself is the brand's strongest visual asset.
