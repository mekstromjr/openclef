# Brand

OpenClef is a self-hosted sheet music library for families and musicians.
Its identity, **"The Grand Manuscript,"** balances the heritage of printed
music with the calm efficiency of a well-run homelab. The brand should
feel professional without feeling corporate, modern without feeling
sterile, and personal without feeling precious.

## Tagline

- **Primary:** *New Tools for a Timeless Medium.*
- **Secondary (product-marketing):** *Own your library. Open your music.*

## Brand pillars

| Pillar | What it means | What it rules out |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Open** | Self-hosted, OFL fonts, source-available, no lock-in. The user owns their files. | Closed formats, "cloud-only," dark patterns, walled gardens. |
| **Human** | Built for real families and musicians. Warm copy, generous whitespace, real instruments in imagery. | Corporate-stock cheer, AI-stock clichés, hostile empty states. |
| **Lasting** | A library you'll still be using in twenty years. Stable formats (PDF, MusicXML), durable visual language, no trend-chasing. | Throwaway design fads, churny rebrands, ephemeral file formats. |
